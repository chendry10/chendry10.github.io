# Cinematic art

Created using the built-in image_gen tool for this portfolio revision. The tool does not report its model name or dollar cost. Full prompts are retained in prompts.json. These are illustrative generated music/keyboard scenes, not photographs of Cameron's work or actual product screens.

Native outputs are 1672 pixels wide and approximately941pixels high. They are intentionally not described as native4K or HDR. Runtime WebPs preserve their original pixel dimensions; mobile versions are reduced to1280pixels wide. No artificial enlargement is presented as added detail. The original PNG outputs are preserved in local qa/cinematic-originals (excluded from standalone publication exports).

Scene1: gold trumpet in cobalt optical glass and warm caustics. Scene2: warm brass in a copper concert hall. Scene3: exploded three-valve macro study with optical prism. Scene4: brass suspended over teal acoustic ripples. Scene5: tactile keyboard macro with copper mechanisms and refracted light.

The exact LyreLink demo, original music pages and authentic education-product captures remain separate assets. Descriptions of Cameron's engineering refer to those actual products; the generated artwork is thematic presentation.
